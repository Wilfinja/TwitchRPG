Twitch RPG Panel Extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Overview

This Twitch panel extension lets viewers join a broadcaster's livestream RPG game as playable characters. To do that, it links your Twitch identity to a game character and persists your progress across sessions.

This policy explains exactly what data is collected, why, and where it goes.

What We Collect

Via Twitch Identity Linking (ID Share), with your explicit consent:

  • Your numeric Twitch user ID
  • Your Twitch display name

Generated by the game itself:

  • Character stats (level, class, HP, and related attributes)
  • Inventory and equipped items
  • Active status effects and combat history
  • Expedition participation records

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any real-world personal data.

How It's Used

Your Twitch ID and display name are used only to associate you with your RPG character. Game data is used to:

  • Display your character in the broadcaster's game
  • Persist your progress between streams
  • Sync your state to the broadcaster's game client in real time

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for analytics of any kind.

Identity Linking

This extension uses Twitch's Identity Linking (ID Share) feature. You will be shown a consent prompt before your Twitch ID is shared. You can decline — the panel will display in a limited state if you do.

Where Data Is Stored

Character data is stored on a backend server controlled by the broadcaster, hosted on Railway. Data is persisted to disk on that server. It is not transmitted to or shared with any third party.

No data is stored in your browser's local storage or sent to Anthropic, Twitch, or any other service beyond what Twitch's own Extension API requires to function.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your character data persists for as long as the game is active. To request deletion of your data, contact the broadcaster directly via Twitch.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to the broadcaster on Twitch.
